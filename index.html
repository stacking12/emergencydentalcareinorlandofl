Emergency Dental Care FAQs

What counts as emergency dental care?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out, broken or chipped tooth, swelling or a dental abscess, bleeding that will not stop, and injury to the mouth or jaw all call for emergency dental care.

Can I get a same day dental appointment in Orlando?

Yes. Call 407-220-1474 and we will arrange an urgent dental appointment as quickly as possible, so you do not have to wait days in pain.

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently with water without scrubbing, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call us right away, because treatment within an hour gives the best chance of saving it.

Do you offer affordable emergency dentist services?

We aim to keep emergency dental visits affordable and will explain your treatment options and costs before we begin. Call us to ask about payment and insurance.

Is a dental abscess an emergency?

Yes. An abscess is an infection that can spread. If you have a painful swelling on your gum or face, get dental abscess treatment promptly. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.
